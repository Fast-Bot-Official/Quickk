Privacy Policy

Please read and scroll to the bottom to continue.

1. Anonymous Authentication: Quickk! uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication. Your account is linked to a system UID, but no personal information (email, phone, real name) is strictly required.


2. Anonymous ID & Profile: You are assigned an 8-digit unique ID. Your display name, bio, and profile picture are visible to anyone with whom you communicate or who searches your ID.


3. Media, Groups & Messages: Messages and group configurations are stored in Firebase Realtime Database. Media attachments (images, voice, videos) are uploaded via Cloudinary. Uploaded media is subject to size restrictions (Images/Voice < 1MB, Video < 20MB, Avatars < 2MB).


4. Experimental Voice/Video Calls: Calls utilize WebRTC signaling via Firebase. Calls are peer-to-peer; Quickk! does not record call audio/video content.


5. Emergency Use Notice: Quickk! is a peer-to-peer anonymous messaging service. It DOES NOT replace official emergency responders (911, 112, 100, etc.). If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services immediately.

Safety & Anonymity

• Internal Identity: Your account is backed by an internal Firebase UID, but users only identify you through your 8-digit Quickk! ID.

• Data Storage: Messages remain safely in Firebase RTDB, and images/voice/videos are processed through Cloudinary unsigned pipelines.

• Group Moderation: Group owners and admins can remove disruptive members and moderate content.

• Blocking & Reporting: Block troublesome users or report illegal content directly to moderation logs.

• Emergency Notice: Quickk! is not a substitute for local police, ambulance, or emergency rescue services.
