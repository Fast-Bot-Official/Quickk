Privacy Policy

Please read and scroll to the bottom to continue.

1. Anonymous Authentication: Quickk! uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication linked to a system UID.


2. Anonymous ID & Profile: You are assigned an 8-digit unique ID visible across direct chats and public groups.


3. Media & Messaging 2.0: Direct messages, groups, videos, polls, and WebRTC signal logs are stored in Firebase RTDB and Cloudinary.


4. Experimental Calling & One-Time Media: Voice/Video calls and View-Once media are provided on a best-effort basis without absolute untraceability guarantees.


5. Emergency Use Notice: Quickk! DOES NOT replace official emergency responders (911, 112, 100, etc.). Contact local authorities immediately in life-threatening situations.
