      Privacy Policy

      1. Instant Emergency Messaging Purpose
      Quickk! is designed specifically for fast, zero-friction emergency messaging between individuals using temporary anonymous accounts.


      2. Data We Collect
      We only collect temporary session identifiers, an automatically generated 8-digit friend code, display names, and messages transmitted. Images and voice audio are stored transiently.


      3. Automatic Account Deletion
      Your anonymous account and session state are temporary. When you click "Logout", your anonymous account and associated local data are deleted immediately and automatically from our servers.


      4. Security & Isolation
      Chats are private between friend codes. Blocking prevents any state or message synchronization between users.
