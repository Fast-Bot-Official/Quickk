App Privacy & Data Policy

Purpose of Quickk!: Quickk! is created strictly for instant, fast, and lightweight emergency messaging between anonymous users utilizing 8-digit friend codes.

Data Collected:

  • Random 8-Digit Code: Assigned randomly by JavaScript to route your messages.
  • Display Name: Selected by you to identify yourself in chats.
  • Presence & Typing Status: Realtime online/offline and active typing indicators.
  • Transmitted Messages: Text, images (under 3MB), and voice notes sent during active conversations.

Data Management & Auto-Deletion:

All accounts are completely anonymous. To safeguard emergency data privacy, your anonymous account and associated RTDB data will automatically delete permanently upon logging out.
