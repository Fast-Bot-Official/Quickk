Privacy & Data Usage

Purpose of the App: Quickk! is designed specifically for instant emergency messaging requiring immediate, anonymous communication.


Data Collected:

  • Random 8-digit friend code assigned to your session.
  • Display Name set by you.
  • Real-time online status, last seen timestamp, and typing indicators.
  • Messages, images (<3MB), and voice notes sent during active conversations.

Account Deletion Notice: Your anonymous account and associated profile data will be permanently deleted automatically whenever you log out of the platform.
